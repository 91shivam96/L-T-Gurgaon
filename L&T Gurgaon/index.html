L&T Gurgaon Project Overview

L&T Realty, the real estate arm of Larsen & Toubro, is entering the Gurugram residential market with a new development in Sector 86, New Gurugram. The company secured the site by acquiring a 100% stake in International Green Scapes Ltd. (IGSL), gaining access to a 20 acre land parcel with a development potential of around 3.6 million sq. ft. This is L&T Realty's first land acquisition in the Gurgaon region.

The location offers strong connectivity, with access to NH-48, Dwarka Expressway and the Southern Peripheral Road (SPR), linking the site to key parts of Gurugram and the wider Gurgaon. Some industry reports suggest the project will be positioned in the premium or luxury residential segment, though L&T Realty has not confirmed this officially yet.

At present, the project is still in its pre-launch stage. Details like the number of towers, unit sizes, pricing, possession timeline and RERA registration haven't been announced. Buyers and investors should wait for L&T's official disclosures rather than relying on early or unverified information.

What Makes L&T Gurgaon Different?

L&T Gurgaon is different mainly because of the scale of the development, the developer's first Gurgaon entry, and the size of the proposed residences. L&T Realty's Gurugram project is its first residential land acquisition in the Gurgaon and covers approximately 20 acres, with a development potential of around 3.6 million sq. ft. The proposed layouts are also relatively spacious, with current project information indicating 3 BHK residences of around 2,500 and 2,800 sq. ft. and 4 BHK homes of around 4,000 sq. ft. Phase 1 is reported to have four apartments per core, which means fewer residences may share common lift and lobby areas. Another difference is the location. Sector 86 has access to NH-48, Dwarka Expressway and SPR, giving the project connectivity to multiple major routes rather than depending on a single corridor. For buyers comparing projects, these are the factors that make L&T Gurgaon worth evaluating alongside other large residential developments in New Gurugram.
